Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying, renewing, and filing insurance claims.

What is insurance and why do I need it?
Insurance is a financial safety contract between you and an insurer. It protects you from heavy financial losses caused by illness, accidents, property damage, or natural calamities by providing reimbursement and cash payouts.
How do I buy an insurance policy online?
Buying a policy takes under 5 minutes. Select your required coverage, fill out basic contact & health details, review your instant quote, and complete payment. Your policy document will be generated instantly in your online vault.
How can I file a claim with Stackly Insurance?
Log into your User Dashboard, navigate to 'My Claims', click 'File a Claim', upload bills/photos of the incident, and submit. Our claims review engine processes and approves verified claims within hours.
Can I renew my policy online?
Yes! You can enable auto-renewal or complete one-click renewal directly from your customer dashboard with zero paperwork or medical re-examinations.
What documents are required to get started?
Basic identification (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport), proof of address, and vehicle registration papers (for motor insurance). No paper copies needed — simply upload digital scans or photos.
