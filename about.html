Who We Are

A Modern Insurance Partner You Can Count On

Founded in 2020, Stackly Insurance was created to eliminate red tape, hidden fees, and slow claim processing in traditional insurance.

We combine cutting-edge technology with deep actuarial risk management to deliver instant digital policies, automated claim settlements, and empathetic customer care when you need it most.

Our Mission

To provide clear, affordable, and instant insurance coverage to every individual, family, and business without friction or paperwork.

Our Vision

To become India’s most trusted digital insurance ecosystem, setting global benchmarks in claim speed and customer satisfaction.

Our Core Values

Integrity, complete transparency, customer-first innovation, rapid claim resolution, and unwavering empathy in times of crisis.
